Summary
When creating a Primary Unit (e.g., Piece, Kg, Liter, Roll): Leave Base Unit empty (None).
When creating a Derived / Sub-unit (e.g., Dozen, Pack, Gram, Sack, Box): Select the Base Unit and enter the Multiplier (Conversion Rate).
When selling at the POS counter or buying from wholesalers, the system automatically uses this multiplier to keep your stock 100% accurate!
